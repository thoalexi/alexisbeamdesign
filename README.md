# AlexisBeamDesign

PROJECT: ALEXIS BEAM
Personal Brand, Web Design Portfolio & Digital Solutions

Build a beautiful, sophisticated, fully responsive portfolio website for Alexis Beam, an independent web designer and digital solutions consultant.

The website should feel like a premium boutique creative studio rather than a generic freelancer portfolio or corporate technology consulting agency.

The primary goal is to convert visitors into prospective clients by showcasing Alexis's existing projects, demonstrating her unique professional background, and making it incredibly easy to inquire about working together.

BRAND IDENTITY

Business Name: Alexis Beam
Descriptor: Web Design & Digital Solutions
Primary Tagline: Thoughtfully designed. Purposefully built.

Brand Positioning:
Beautiful, functional websites and creative digital solutions for small businesses, entrepreneurs, and people with big ideas.

Brand Personality:
- Sophisticated but approachable
- Intelligent but never pretentious
- Creative but intentional
- Professional without being corporate
- Warm, welcoming, and personable
- Modern and quietly confident

The brand should communicate that clients receive the creativity of a designer, the analytical thinking of a strategist, and the personal attention of an independent professional.

IMPORTANT DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a sophisticated, editorial-style website inspired by premium independent design studios.

The website should feel expensive, considered, and effortlessly beautiful.

Avoid generic SaaS website aesthetics.

Do not use:
- Generic purple-to-blue gradients
- Stock photographs of people in offices
- Excessive rounded cards
- Generic AI-generated illustrations
- Overused technology icons
- Corporate dashboard aesthetics
- Unnecessary visual clutter

Instead, emphasize:
- Beautiful typography
- Intentional whitespace
- Asymmetrical but balanced layouts
- Elegant editorial composition
- Subtle interactions
- High-quality project previews
- Sophisticated color combinations
- Smooth scrolling
- Strong visual hierarchy

COLOR PALETTE

Use a refined, Mediterranean-inspired palette:

Primary background: Warm ivory (#F8F6F1)
Primary text: Deep charcoal (#252525)
Accent: Muted Aegean blue (#547780)
Secondary accent: Warm terracotta (#C88E76)
Secondary background: Soft cream (#EFECE5)
White: #FFFFFF

Use color sparingly and intentionally.

The overall aesthetic should be approximately 80% neutral, with carefully selected accent colors.

TYPOGRAPHY

Use an elegant combination of serif and sans-serif typography.

Suggested:
- Headlines: Cormorant Garamond or a similarly beautiful editorial serif
- Body: DM Sans or a similar modern sans-serif

The typography should be a defining element of the website.

Large, expressive headlines.
Beautiful spacing.
Clean, highly readable body text.

Create subtle contrast between oversized serif headlines and smaller modern sans-serif supporting text.

LOGO

Create an elegant text-based logo:

alexis beam.

Use lowercase lettering for the primary wordmark.

Explore a small custom monogram using the letters AB that could be used as a favicon.

The logo should feel minimalist, elegant, and timeless.
