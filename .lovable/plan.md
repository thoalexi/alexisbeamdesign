## Section-by-section changes

1. **Inquiries submitted through this website** (renamed from "Information you submit"). It lists the same fields as today, and its claims get a narrower scope:
   > "I use website inquiries to review your request and respond about your project. I don't sell this information, add you to a marketing email list, or use your form answers for promotional marketing."
   
   The absolute line "I don't use it for advertising" is removed.

2. **How inquiries are processed.** The current text stays, with one line added:
   > "The website database and notification-email process described here applies to inquiries submitted through this website."

3. **New section: "Facebook and Instagram consultation requests".** This is your wording, stating the confirmed path directly: requests are collected through Meta and automatically transferred to a Google Sheet I use to manage consultation requests. It links to Meta's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy, both labeled "opens in a new tab". It doesn't name the spreadsheet or tab, and makes no access or security claims.

4. **New section: "Advertising measurement with Meta".**
   > "My Facebook and Instagram consultation request forms are connected to Meta's Conversions API, a tool Meta provides for measuring how my ads perform. Meta processes information it receives under its own privacy policy. I don't use this connection to build marketing lists or send you promotional messages."
   
   Below it, a visible marker reads: "Unresolved — to verify before publication: what this connection sends to Meta." It makes no claims about anonymity, hashing, events, identifiers or lead-status changes.

5. **Analytics.** The current text stays, with one sentence added:
   > "Google Analytics is separate from the Google Sheet described above. Form responses are not sent to Google Analytics."

6. **Service providers.** Two providers are added:
   - Meta Platforms: Facebook and Instagram lead forms, plus the connected ad-measurement tool.
   - Google Sheets: storing and managing Meta consultation requests.

7. **Your choices and questions.** This section now also covers Meta requests and their copies in Google Sheets:
   > "Information may be held in both Meta and Google Sheets. Contact me about access or deletion requests."
   
   The contact email stays the same.

8. **Retention.** Website inquiries keep their current wording. For Meta and Google Sheets, a visible marker reads: "Unresolved — retention awaiting confirmation."

9. The "Changes to this policy" section stays the same.
